# Farm greybox

A walkable greybox of the farm, built from plain blocks, for testing the walk-first controls and the core loop before any art. It follows the vault notes *15 3D World & Avatar Play*, *09 Roblox Architecture*, *02 Farming*, *05 Orders & Economy* and *Township Baseline Numbers*.

## What's in it

- **World:** a hub with a spawn, a Ring Road, and 12 plot slots around it. Each player gets a plot (only the 96 x 96 stud start area is built) with 6 fields (2 x 2 tiles), a Barn, a row of 5 factories, 3 animal pens, an Order Board with a delivery truck, and a gate with your name on it.
- **Walk-first controls ("Reach + Errands"):**
  - Tap or click anything on your plot within **Reach (32 studs)** and it happens. Things in reach get an outline.
  - Tap something farther away and your avatar **auto-walks** there at sprint speed, then does it. Steering yourself cancels the walk.
  - **Hold the action button** (E, gamepad X, or the round touch button) to sweep: it harvests ready beds in front of you and sows your last seed into empty beds you walk over. The button's label shows the current "tool" (Scythe, Seeds, Use).
  - **Walk past** a factory's output table to collect finished goods.
  - **Tap a pen** (or hold the action button next to it) to collect what the animals made; collecting refeeds them if you have feed. Tapping a pen with nothing to do opens its panel.
  - Walk 20, sprint 28 studs/s (Shift, left stick click, or the Sprint button). Camera zoom 10 to 45.
- **Core loop:** plant crops, timers keep running while you're away, harvest into the Barn (50 slots; 1 unit a field, as in Township), feed animals, build factories and make goods, fill Order Board cards for Coins and XP, level up to the live cap (12 at launch, see the content bank below). Level-ups hand over a few units of the new crop or feed (Township: 4 Corn at 3, 4 Carrots at 4, 3 Chicken Feed at 5, 3 Sugarcane at 7; Strawberries and Tomatoes later). The Barn can sell items.
- **Everything in the level-12 MVP** (*18 MVP & Roadmap*):
  - Crops: Wheat (L1), Corn (L3), Carrots (L4), Sugarcane (L7), Cotton (L9).
  - Animals: the Cow Barn comes with 3 Cows at level 1 (plus 9 bags of Cow Feed to start, Township's level-1 gift), Chicken Coop at 5, Sheep Fold at 10. Each pen comes with 3 animals and you can buy up to 3 more. One bag of feed feeds 3 animals.
  - Factories: Bakery (L2), Feed Mill (L3), Dairy (L4), Sugar Mill (L7), Textile Mill (L9) and Tailor (L12, in the East Meadow, Township's Tailor Shop), with 13 recipes: Bread, Cookies, Bagel; Cow, Chicken and Sheep Feed (3 bags a batch); Cream, Cheese, Butter; Sugar; Cotton Fabric, Yarn; Shirt.
  - Order cards only ask for things your farm can make right now (and never feed).
- **Gem speed-ups** (Township baseline prices, two taps to confirm): finish a growing field (tap it; with 2+ fields of that crop growing, **Finish all** does them together for the summed price), a factory job or construction, every producing animal in a pen, or an Order Board wait; buy extra factory queue slots (8 Gems, then +4 each, to 6 slots; the 7th waits for factory upgrades). Finished goods wait on a 6-item output tray (Township start; upgrades would take it to 8).
- **Town** (*07 Town Building*, Township baseline): Houses raise Population, Community Buildings raise the Population Cap (base 75, Township: the Houses that unlock always fill it exactly). A farm starts with 4 Houses (Population 60). House copies unlock by level and need room under the Cap; factories need a minimum Population to build (Bakery 55 … Textile Mill 105). Community Buildings also cost Bricks, Planks and Panes, which drop from harvests (2%, more for long crops) and Order Board deliveries (25%) from level 5, or cost 8 Gems each (drop rates first-pass). There is no builder limit: any number of Houses, Community Buildings, factories and pens can be under construction at once, as in Township (decided 2026-10-30). New buildings go on the next free lot of **Main Street**, the strip between your gate and the Ring Road, then of **North Lane** in the North Fields. Tap any town building, or press T, for the Town panel.
- **More fields and land** (*02 Farming*, Township baseline): fields 7 to 22 arrive free as Population grows (65 up to 360), with XP. Fields 7 to 10 fit in the start area; from field 11 on they need the West Meadow cleared. Plots are 288 studs across (Township-sized land): the 96-stud start area, Main Street, and 14 land expansions on Township's table (Population 60 to 280, 25 to 190 Coins, 20 minutes to 1 h 25, no tools before #20), each needing the previous one finished. The first four (West Meadow, East Meadow, North Fields, Far Corners) surround the start area; expansions 5 to 14 (West and East Woods, the Gate Meadows, the Southwest and Southeast Hollows, North Ridge, the Northwest and Northeast Hills) make the outer ring, with sparser wild obstacles for the part budget. The hub grew to match (cells of 320 studs). Start them from the Land section of the Town panel.
- **Barn upgrades** (*02 Farming*, Township baseline): upgrade *n* costs *n* each of Nails, Hinges and Paint and no Coins (50 → 70 → 90 → 110, then +25 each). Barn materials drop from harvests and orders from level 5 on the same roll as building materials, or cost 12 Gems each. When the Barn is full (less than 2 free), its panel says so; it always lists what you have most of first, with Sell buttons.
- **First-session guide** (*11 UI & UX* FTUE, *01 Core Loop*, the first-60-seconds script in *17*): new farms start with 3 ripe Wheat fields and an easy 3-Wheat order. A card at the bottom with a townsfolk guide (Hattie, Pip, Auntie Crumb, Mort) gives one line per step and a bouncing arrow points at what to walk to, through the first 30 minutes: harvest, replant, ship the first truck, reach level 2, build the Bakery, bake Bread, feed the Cows, collect Milk, reach level 3, build the Feed Mill and make Cow Feed, reach level 4, build a Lean-to Cabin, build the Dairy (its first Finish is free), start the West Meadow, reach level 5, then 5 Gems. The first Wheat, Bread and Milk while their step is on take 30 s. Steps end on their own when their goal is met; Skip appears from step 3. Each step is logged to the Roblox onboarding funnel. Farms from before the guide don't see it; the Debug panel can replay it.
- **Coming back** (*01 Core Loop*, *08 Social & Events*): after 5+ minutes away, a "While you were away" panel lists ready fields, goods waiting, animal products, buildings and land that finished, and the orders on the board. From level 5 a 7-day login calendar gives a gift on each new UTC day (100 Coins, Nails, 200 Coins, Hinges, 5 Gems, 300 Coins + Paint, 15 Gems; Coins x1.5 from level 11). A missed day starts the round again from day 1 (Township). Tool and decoration days give Barn materials until those ship. The Debug panel can fake an absence.
- **Daily Quests** (*08 Social & Events*, first-pass numbers): once the first-session guide is done, three quests a day from 00:00 UTC, picked from what your farm can do: harvest 30 crops (80 Coins, 20 XP), collect 10 animal goods (80, 20), make 5 factory goods (100, 25) or deliver 3 orders (120, 30); targets are halved below level 5. Progress counts on its own and a toast says when one is done. Claim rewards in the Quests panel (Q, or the 📜 button, which shows a red dot when something is ready); one free swap a day for an unfinished quest. Claiming all three opens a bonus chest: 3 to 10 Gems, or from level 5 a 30% chance of your scarcest tool. Logged as `DailyQuest` and `QuestChest` SKUs plus a `DailyQuestDone` custom event.
- **Seed Cart** (*13 Roblox Success Mechanics* §3.7, roadmap week 3; first-pass prices): a cart on the Town Square's east side that restocks every 30 minutes at :00 and :30 UTC, the same on every server, with the countdown on its sign. Tap it for four slots, Coins only, one of each per restock: two crates of 5 of a crop you've unlocked (1.5× the sell price, straight into the Barn), a decoration at 25% off (prepaid: the next one you place is free) and, in about a quarter of restocks, an **Heirloom Seed** (60 Coins: the next field you plant has 10× the Heirloom chance); otherwise a third crate. Opens at level 3. Held off until its update (`Config/SeedCart.enabled`), always on in Studio. Logged as the `SeedCart` SKU.
- **Decorations** (*07 Town Building*, Township baseline): 12 Coin decorations (paths, fences, hedges, flower beds, trees, hay bales, lamp posts, benches, picnic tables, a pond and a fountain) unlock from level 2 to 10 and give a little XP. Buy one from the Town panel and a green ghost appears in front of you; walk to move it, Turn to rotate it, Place to put it down. It snaps to a 4-stud grid, must sit on your own land (the start area or a cleared expansion) and can't overlap a building, field or another decoration (the ghost turns red). Tap a placed decoration to turn it or sell it back for 50%. Up to 60 per farm, 12 of them big (2 x 2 tiles and up), to keep the plot inside the part budget.
- **Move mode** (the free-placement sandbox, decided 2026-10-31; `shared/Layout`, `LayoutService`, `Controllers/MovePlacer`): the HUD's Move button (or M) turns it on, and tapping a field, factory, pen, House or Community Building picks it up. Its footprint ghost follows your avatar on a 2-stud grid, green where it fits and red where it doesn't (off your land, or something already there), with Place / Turn (quarter turns) / Cancel. The server checks again, saves the spot in `layout[key] = { x, z, r }` and rebuilds the building there; unmoved buildings keep their usual spots. A new town building whose usual lot is now covered by something you moved goes up at the nearest open spot instead (`PlotService.placeNew`). Your land is the start area, Main Street and every cleared expansion. Building something new (a House, Community Building, factory or pen) picks it up in Move mode as soon as it goes up, so you can walk it where you want it (Cancel leaves it where it went up; not during the first-session guide). Buildings no longer wait for a lot or a usual spot: when the House lots are full, a Community Building's lot or a factory's or pen's usual spot is on land not cleared yet, or a new building has no usual spot, it goes up at the nearest open spot to you (`PlotService.prepareBuild` / `findOpenSpot`), and only no room at all stops it.
- **Tool drops** (*02 Farming*): from level 5 each field harvest rolls once on one table for a Shovel (3%), Axe (2%), Saw (1.5%) or a building or Barn material (2% each group), better for long crops (up to x3). After 40 harvests in a row without a tool, the next one gives the tool you have fewest of. Order deliveries also have a 5% chance at the scarcest tool. Tools sit in the Tool Shed at the bottom of the Barn panel and take no Barn space; the four MVP land clearings don't need them yet (Shovels start at expansion 20 on the Township baseline).
- **Prize Crops lite** (*02 Farming*, first-pass): when a crop is planted (so a speed-up never buys a roll), the server rolls for a **Sunkissed** (2%) or **Heirloom** (0.2%) crop; none during the first-session guide. On harvest one unit goes to the 10-slot **Prize Basket** (bottom of the Barn panel, no Barn space) and sells at the Prize Stand for x3 or x15 the crop's price. With the basket full it's an ordinary unit. An Heirloom find is announced to everyone on the server, and every find goes on the **Notice Board** in the Town Square (last 8, this server only). Show up to 3 on the **Prize Shelf** by your Barn (tap Show in the Barn panel; Take down puts it back in the basket). Selling a Prize Crop takes two taps. A Prize Crop in the basket also counts as a normal unit for orders and factories when the Barn is short (Barn units are used first, then the least valuable Prize Crop); shelved ones don't, and Barn Sell never touches them.
- **Shop** (*10 Monetization*, *09 Roblox Architecture* §10; G or the 💎 HUD button): three Gem packs (15 / 31 / 80 Gems, planned at 49 / 99 / 249 R$: Township's rate of about 33 Gems per 100 R$), a one-time Starter Pack from level 5 (50 Gems + a Shovel, an Axe and a Saw, planned at 99 R$) and the **Big Barn** game pass (+50 Barn space). Robux prices are read from Roblox at runtime. Only the server prompts; `ProcessReceipt` grants, records the receipt id (newest 200 kept) and saves before confirming, and rolls back if the save fails so Roblox retries. Product and pass ids are 0 (shown as "Coming soon") until they are created on the Creator Dashboard and filled in `Config/Products.luau`.
- **Analytics** (*19 Analytics & Metrics* §4, through `TelemetryService`, server-side only): every Coins, Gems, XP and Tools change is summed per currency, flow and SKU and sent every 120 s, on level-up and on leaving (rate-limit budget); custom fields are level band, context and cohort tag (`early_payer`), never ids. Custom events: `SessionStart` (minutes away, gap bucket), `SessionEnd` (minutes played, social or solo), `OrderFulfilled`, `DailyQuestDone`, `GemSkipUsed`, `StorageFull` (at most every 5 min), `FactoryBuilt`, `ExpansionUnlocked`, `PurchasePromptResult`. `PlayerLevel` progression (`L07`), the `Shop` funnel (Opened, Prompted, Granted) and the tutorial's onboarding funnel.
- **Town Square and neighbors** (*14 Multiplayer Features* A1, A2, *08 Social & Events*): the middle of the hub is a round plaza with a fountain, benches and three boards: the Notice Board (Prize Crop news), the Neighbors board (everyone on the server and their level) and a Market board placeholder. Friends who join get a plot next to each other. A Friends Nearby column down the left edge shows your friends on the server; tap one to visit their farm. The Neighbors panel (N, the 👋 button or the Neighbors board) lists everyone with their level and where they are, with Visit and Send home buttons, your farm's settings for visitors (Open, Friends, Closed) and helpers (Nobody, Friends, Anyone), the Notice Board's news with Visit buttons, and buttons to the Town Square and home. A Home button shows whenever you're off your farm. The server checks every visit: blocked players (your Roblox block list) and visitors your settings don't allow are moved to the Town Square, and a visitor sent home can't come back for 10 minutes. Avatars don't collide with each other. In Studio everyone counts as a friend so a local 2-player server can test the friends-only rules. Invite uses Roblox's own invite prompt.
- **Puzzle Porch and heart gifts** (*Features/F12 Crop Pop Puzzle*): a little game cabinet under a porch roof on the west side of the Town Square; tap it (you walk over) to open Crop Pop. The farm fires a client-only `ReplicatedStorage.CropPopOpen` BindableEvent that Crop Pop's client listens to. Friends in the Neighbors panel get a ❤ button that sends a Crop Pop heart through Crop Pop's `ServerStorage.CropPopGiftHeart(fromPlayer, toUserId) -> (ok, message?)`; Crop Pop owns the daily limit and the pending gift.
- **Helping Hands** (*14 Multiplayer Features* B1, MVP scope: water and harvest): from level 3, tap a field on a neighbor's farm (you walk to it first) to harvest it if it's ready, or water it if it's growing. Watering takes 10% off the time left, once per crop, and the soil darkens. Everything harvested goes to the owner's Barn (refused if it's full). The owner's Helpers setting (default Friends) and both players' block lists decide who may help. The game pays the helper, never the owner: 1 Coin and 1 XP for watering, 10% of the field's sell value (at least 1 Coin) and the crop's XP for harvesting, and a 2% chance of their scarcest tool; when the owner is working their own farm at the same time both get +5 XP (Teamwork). One daily budget per helper: 40 actions, at most 15 on one farm (the 🤝 counter shows what's left while you visit). Rewards need a Roblox account at least 3 days old. Studio skips the account-age and level gates.
- **Good Neighbors and the Welcome Boost** (*14 Multiplayer Features* G1, G3): each friend on the server who is level 5+ and did something in the last 5 minutes adds +5% Coins to your Order Board deliveries (at most +10%); a green chip shows it. When a friend joins you (following you into the game, or from your Invite through Roblox's Friend Referral, `ReferredByPlayerId`, or the invite's LaunchData), you both get a 15-minute **Welcome Boost**: crops you plant grow 10% faster. The joiner always gets it; the friend they joined gets it when the joiner is level 5+, and each pair gets it once per UTC day. Inviting itself earns nothing. In Studio the second player to join counts as joining the first.

- **Quarry rules** (*Features/F01 Quarry & Smelter*, Township's mine on the Township baseline): `shared/Quarry` and `Config/Quarry`; `QuarryService` runs it, `Art/Quarry` builds the boarded-up hillside at the north end of the East Meadow's third column (land 2), and the Quarry panel (`UI/Quarry`, opened by walking up to the hill) reopens it and digs the shaft as a 2D grid, like Township's mine. The 3D walk-in shaft from F01 comes later. The Quarry reopens at level 21 (3,000 Coins, 1 day 6 h, 128 XP) and gives 30 Pickaxes. Its shaft is 12 cells wide, and every cell's block (Soil, Stone that takes two swings, or Bedrock that can't be dug and never sits beside another) and find (Copper, Silver, Gold or Platinum Ore deeper down, Township's own four tiers, a pile of Coins, or a Lost Lunchbox of Pickaxes) is fixed by a seed in the save, so reconnecting can't reroll a dig. A cell can be dug once a side neighbour is open; one swing costs one Pickaxe. Ore goes to the Ore Shed (`oreShed`, no cap, never the Barn) and Pickaxes live in the Tool Shed. 5 free Pickaxes a day at the gate. The save fields `quarry`, `oreShed` and `buildingLevels` (for the Tinker's Guild) are reserved now. Ore depths and drop rates are first-pass (the wiki doesn't list them).

- **Airship Freight** (*Features/F04*, Township's airport and airplane): `Config/Blimp`, `shared/Blimp`, `BlimpService`, `Art/Blimp` and the Blimp Mast panel (`UI/Blimp`, opened by tapping the freight office). A Cargo Blimp then docks with 3 rows of 2-3 crates of goods the farm can make (crate sizes from the wiki's Airport Quantities, growing with level). A crate pays Coins and XP when loaded (tap it under the blimp, or Load / Buy missing in the panel); sending pays each full row's Coins and Sky Miles, and loading every crate adds the Full Hold chest (a Bar, a tool stack and a material stack, fixed when the load is drawn and shown up front). Next Flight shows the next load's goods while the blimp is away. Studio: the Dev tab's Airship Freight button repairs the mast and docks a blimp now. Help flags: the owner can ask for help with one crate a row (Ask for help in the panel). Anyone on the server who may help on that farm (the owner's Helpers setting, block lists and the Helping Hands daily budget, `HelpService.blimp`) can walk up and tap the flagged crate to load it from their own Barn: the crate counts for the owner, and the game pays the helper the crate's Coins, XP and Sky Miles. Flagged crates carry a red flag and a "Help: 12 Milk" label, and the Neighbors panel lists every farm asking, with a Visit button (the owner's `BlimpHelp` attribute). Reward multipliers are first-pass.

## Numbers

Config lives in `src/shared/Config/`. Crops, animals and pens, factories, recipes, the XP curve, level rewards, the starting wallet (500 Coins, 30 Gems), the Barn size and the 30-minute order refresh are the **Township baseline**. Order slots per level, the order reward formula and all the walking numbers are **first-pass** and meant to be tuned in this greybox.

What's switched on is in `Balance.enabledCrops`, `Balance.enabledFactories` and `Balance.enabledPens`; the full crop table is already in the config. The 9 bags of starter Cow Feed are Township's level-1 gift.

## Run it in Studio

With [Rokit](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rokit) installed, from this folder:

```sh
rokit install
rojo serve
```

Then connect with the Rojo plugin in a new Baseplate place. The game builds its own ground and removes the template's Baseplate and SpawnLocation when the server starts. Or build a place file with `rojo build default.project.json -o farm-greybox.rbxlx` and open it.

The project maps to:

| Folder | Goes to |
|---|---|
| `src/shared` | `ReplicatedStorage.FarmShared` |
| `../uitheme/src` | `ReplicatedStorage.UiTheme` (shared UI look, see Design/25 UI Style Guide) |
| `src/server` | `ServerScriptService.FarmServer` (`Main` is the Script) |
| `src/client` | `StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts.FarmClient` (`Main` is the LocalScript) |

Shared modules use Luau's string requires (`require("./Config/Crops")`) so the same files run in Roblox and in the Lune tests.

**Saving in Studio:** turn on *Game Settings > Security > Enable Studio Access to API Services* to test real saves (they go to a separate `_DEV` store). Without it, the game warns and plays on an unsaved farm.

**Screen tour (Studio only, `]` key):** steps through the HUD alone, Barn, Orders, Shop, Buildings, Town, the seed picker for field 1 and Debug, printing `[Tour] n/8 <screen> at <width>x<height>` to Output. Set the device emulator to a phone first to check the mobile layout.

**Debug panel (Studio only, `` ` `` key):** try Reach 0 / 16 / 32 / 48, walk speed 16 / 20 / 24, and shorter timers (x0.25 makes Wheat take 30 s, as in the prototype checklist). Its buttons also replay the guide, fake an absence, build everything, grant +500 Gems, +5,000 Coins and 5 of each tool, give **+1 level** (which also opens the content bank's levels on that Studio server), raise Town Records to their next medals, and **fuzz every remote** (*09* §12 exploit checks): each client-to-server handler gets junk arguments (NaN, infinities, negative and fractional numbers, empty and 10,000-character strings, tables, an Instance), first in every slot and then one slot at a time, about 3,500 calls. Output lists every handler that threw (a bug: its checks should reject first) and every call that returned ok (check by hand). The farm is restored afterwards. Remotes with no arguments, Shop prompts and avatar moves are skipped.

## Tests

```sh
lune run tests/run.luau     # config integrity, timers, levels, order generation, saves, compile check
stylua --check src tests    # formatting
```

CI runs both and a Rojo build on every pull request that touches `farm/`.

Data safety (`tests/datasafety.luau`): a played farm touching every save block, Crop Pop's included, must pass `SaveCheck` and come back from JSON (what a DataStore keeps) unchanged. Every past save version has a fixture in `tests/fixtures/` that must load into today's layout. When a migration lands, add the old version's fixture. On every save the server runs `SaveCheck` and, if something can't be stored (NaN, an array with holes, number keys in a dictionary, a cycle, over 4 MB), warns with the exact field.
